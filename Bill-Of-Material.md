## Panels

Panel material selection depends on chamber temperature, mounting, and service life requirements. Common options:

- Double-walled Polycarbonate (PC): This provides the best thermal insulation compared to single solid panels. Used for builds where reduced heat loss and better enclosure temperature stability are required. Both inner and outer layers must be sealed and mounted to prevent moisture ingress and maintain insulation. Double all panel quantities for this variant.
- Polycarbonate (PC): **Recommended for heated chambers** and higher service temperatures. Good impact resistance. Continuous service temperature 115–120 °C. May creep above 90–100 °C under load. UV-stabilized grades available. See [Where to get panels](Where-to-get-panels.md) for grades and sourcing.
- Acrylic (PMMA): Suitable for **non-heated** builds. Lower cost, higher scratch resistance, but lower service temperature (70–80 °C) and more brittle than PC. Cast acrylic preferred for optical clarity.

Always confirm panel thickness, span, and mounting method against the current CAD and [Where to get panels](Where-to-get-panels.md) before ordering. For detailed thermal, mechanical, and fabrication notes, see the full guide.
